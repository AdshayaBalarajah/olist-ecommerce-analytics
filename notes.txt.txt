OLIST PROJECT NOTES

DATA CLEANING / CHECKS
- 9 tables loaded into PostgreSQL; orders = 99,441, no duplicate order IDs
- 97% of orders are delivered (96,478); analysis uses delivered orders only
- 2,965 orders have no delivery date (mostly canceled / not delivered)
- 610 products have no category -> labelled "unknown"
- Dates loaded as text -> converted with ::timestamp
- 2016 data is incomplete (266 orders in total) -> trends use Jan 2017 to Aug 2018
- Used customer_unique_id (not customer_id) to identify repeat customers
- Monthly revenue uses payment value (includes freight); category and state
  revenue use item price (excludes freight), so don't compare them directly

KEY FINDINGS
1. Orders grew from 750 (Jan 2017) to 7,069 (Jan 2018), about 9x; revenue
   grew about 8.5x (R$127.5K to R$1.08M)
2. Peak in Nov 2017: 7,289 orders and R$1.15M (Black Friday); Dec 2017 fell
   25% to 5,513 orders
3. Growth flattened in 2018: about 6,000-7,000 orders and R$1.0M-R$1.13M a month
4. Average order value: R$159.86
5. Top categories: health_beauty (R$1.23M), watches_gifts (R$1.17M),
   bed_bath_table (R$1.02M); the top 10 are about 62% of item revenue
6. SP = 38% of revenue; SP + RJ + MG = 63%; northern states (RR, AP, AC)
   are under R$20K each
7. Repeat-customer rate is only 3.00% (2,801 of 93,358 customers)
8. 8.11% of deliveries were late (7,826 of 96,470)

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Retention program (loyalty offers, follow-up emails): 97% buy only once
2. Focus marketing on SP, RJ, MG; investigate why other regions are low
3. Investigate causes of late deliveries, especially in distant states
4. Plan stock and promotions around the November peak

CV BULLET DRAFTS

  using SQL (PostgreSQL), covering revenue trends, categories, regions
  and delivery performance
- Found that only 3% of customers made repeat purchases and 8% of orders
  arrived late, and recommended a retention program and a delivery review
